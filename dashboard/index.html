Every term, held out four ways

Each row is one model. Leave-one-out prices each launch from the other 94. Five-fold splits the launches by size into five folds and prices each fold from the other four. Walk-forward fits on the older half of dated launches and prices the newer half — the hardest test, because the future really is unseen. Category-blind fits without one group at a time and prices that group. A term is kept only when it lowers the miss in both headline tests (leave-one-out and walk-forward).

How honest is the range?

The range on every row comes from the model's own misses: the middle 50% of launched tokens landed between 0.34× and 2.84× their fitted price, the middle 80% between 0.08× and 10.80×. A range is only honest if it holds when the model is tested on data it never saw.

The board — 198 protocols, ranked by implied value

Implied value is what the token would be worth if it launched today: value-if-launches × launch odds. Your slice is your share of a 7% airdrop at a 0.02% of TVL plan (1 of 5,000 farmers). $1K slice is the same share on a $1,000 plan. Net is slice minus gas, swap fee and slippage. Break-even is the implied value at which the plan pays exactly zero at this plan size — if the printed implied value is above it, the plan pays at the point value. Verdict: ROBUST pays even at the low end of the printed range; FARM pays at the point value (net ≥ $25 and ≥ $5/hour); THIN only beats its cash fees; SKIP loses more than $50.

Method, limits, and what to check

Method

  1. Snapshot. 758 protocols from DeFiLlama, frozen on 14 Nov 2026. 416 already have a token; 198 are ranked; 144 are set aside by the kit's house rule. 758 = 198 + 416 + 144.
  2. Value. log10(market cap) = a + b × log10(TVL), fitted on the 95 dated peers, times a per-group residual (median of the group's launches, clamped 0.5–2.0), times a 7-day momentum tilt winsorized at the snapshot p99 (+26.7%).
  3. Odds. Logistic regression on 758 protocols: age in years, category group, chain count capped at 6, kinship. Base rate 54.9%.
  4. Implied value. Value-if-launches × launch odds, ranked highest first.
  5. Farm plan. 0.02% of TVL, 7% drop share, 12-week horizon, 3.75 hours of work. Gas for five steps on the protocol's main chain; DEX pairs also pay a 0.60% round-trip swap fee on the half of the plan that must be swapped, plus exit slippage of (half-plan)² / (5% of TVL). Lending, yield and RWA take an asset the farmer already holds, so they pay no swap.
  6. Verdict. ROBUST pays even at the low end of the printed range; FARM pays at the point value (net ≥ $25 and ≥ $5/hour); THIN only beats its cash fees; SKIP loses more than $50. The T-bill comparison uses a 4.5% APY over the 12-week horizon.

Limits — read before you farm anything

  • The curve explains only 16.8% of the variation in launch values (R² 0.168). A typical launch lands 2.87× away from its fitted price; about half land more than 3× away.
  • The snapshot is frozen on 14 Nov 2026. Protocols launched, listed or shut down since then are not in it, and the 7-day momentum is 7 days old.
  • Five of the 40 watchlist entries are listed under different names on DeFiLlama (Aave V3, Lido, Uniswap V3, Spark, Maple); the board matches them by slug and says so in the reconciliation.
  • Kinship is a name match, not proof of a shared team: two protocols can share a first word by coincidence, and the 90% figure comes from only 40 kin protocols.
  • Gas is an estimate ($22 on Ethereum, $3 on a major L2, $6 elsewhere) and moves with the network; the swap fee and slippage are model assumptions, not quotes.
  • A FARM verdict is not a promise. Most airdrops pay less than their model, some pay nothing, and the T-bill comparison in the detail view shows where doing nothing wins.

What to check if you do not trust a number

  1. Open data.js and find the protocol by slug. Every field on its row is there: curve value, group residual, momentum, odds, plan, slice, gas, swap, slippage, net, break-even.
  2. Recompute the implied value by hand: curve × residual × momentum × odds.
  3. Check the checksum in the integrity strip against the one printed in the README. If they differ, the data changed after the build.
  4. Re-run the build: from the work directory, MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node repo/main.ts --input kit/token-radar-01-kit/input/snapshot.json — a second run must be byte-identical.
