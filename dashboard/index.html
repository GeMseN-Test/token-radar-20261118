How the value is priced

ModelLeave-one-out errorLOO within 2×5-fold CV errorCV within 2×

Error = median absolute % error on the 128 live peers (their real market caps); "within 2×" = share of peers priced between half and twice their real cap. Lower error is better. Launch pricing is genuinely dispersed — live mcap/TVL multiples run from 0.01× to 2.97× — so no model here is precise; the kept model wins because it has the lowest error on both tests, and the v1-style category-median model is worst out of sample even though it looks better in-sample (small categories memorise their own median). A single 26-peer holdout was measured first and thrown away: it swings 70–430% between splits for every model — one split is a lottery, not a test. The ridge was tried and rejected: its fitted growth and age coefficients come out negative, pricing fast-growing, long-running protocols down — the opposite of what a launch radar should reward.

Implied value is this radar's own claim from the snapshot's figures — TVL, 7-day growth and time running with no token — not a prediction that a token launches, or when.
