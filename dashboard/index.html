Implied values are this radar's own claim from the snapshot's figures — TVL, seven-day growth, time with no token, group, chain count and name-stem kinship — never a hardcoded list of names. Farm costs are typical gas estimates for the protocol's main chain plus, for DEX pairs only, the swap fee and slippage on the half of the plan that must be swapped. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token is issued.
